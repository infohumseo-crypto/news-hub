SEO Basics: Rank Higher Without Tricks

Oct 14, 2026 · 6 min read

Good SEO is mostly good publishing. Search engines want to send people to pages that answer their question quickly and clearly. Here is what to focus on first.

Match search intent

Before writing, search your target phrase and look at what already ranks. Are people looking for a guide, a list, or a quick answer? Give them that format, only better.

Write clear titles and descriptions

  • Keep the title under about 60 characters and put the main phrase near the start.
  • Write a meta description that promises a specific benefit.
  • Use one <h1> per page and logical subheadings below it.

Keep pages fast and mobile friendly

Most readers arrive on a phone. Compress images, avoid heavy scripts, and test your pages on a slow connection.

Link your own content together

Link related articles to each other with descriptive anchor text. It helps readers discover more and helps search engines understand your site.

Be patient

New sites usually take weeks or months to gain traction. Keep publishing useful posts, update old ones, and measure progress with a free tool like Google Search Console.
