Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This site is a static blog hosted on GitHub Pages. We keep data collection to a minimum.

We do not require an account. If you subscribe to the newsletter or send a message through the contact form, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name and email address.

Your browser stores your light or dark theme choice on your own device. This is not sent to us.

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted by GitHub. These services may collect technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. If analytics or ads are added later, this page will be updated.
